Privacy Policy

Newsroom, Gather and Commons · Effective October 23, 2026

This policy covers three apps that run inside Root communities:

  • Newsroom, an editorial app.
  • Gather, an events calendar.
  • Commons, a wiki.

Each app keeps what it needs to work, inside your community, and nothing more. We built the apps, but we can't see your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no cookies, and nothing is sold or shared.

What the apps keep

In every app: your Root member ID, which shows who did what and gives you the access your community set, and your notification preferences if you change them. Your name and profile picture come from Root. The apps show them but don't store a copy.

Newsroom

Pitches, article texts, comments, suggestions, field values, photos and version history.

Gather

Events and their details, cover images and attached files, who is going, tasks and who they're assigned to, comments, shared notes, the reminders you set and your notifications. It also keeps your language, so notifications reach you in it.

Commons

Pages, their fields, images and files, categories and tags, page history, comments, suggestions, error reports and the pages or categories you follow.

What the apps don't do

The apps don't track you or show ads. Your content is used to run the apps for your community and for nothing else. It isn't sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Links that members add, such as an online meeting link, open other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Where the data lives

Root hosts the apps, their data and their files. Your data stays in your community's copy of each app. Nothing is sent to us or to any other service, and we can't read, export, change or delete it. Root's privacy policy covers your Root account. Your data may be stored outside your country, including in the United States.

Legal basis (GDPR)

The apps use this data to provide the service your community chose (Art. 6(1)(b)). Some features, like version history and reminders, rely on a legitimate interest in tools that work reliably (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

Content stays until your community deletes it. Removing an app from a community removes all of that app's data.

  • Newsroom: deleted text tabs go to a trash and are erased after 30 days by default. Deleting an article erases its texts, comments, history and fields.
  • Gather: deleting an event erases its tasks, comments, notes and who was going. Your notification list keeps your latest 100.
  • Commons: deleting a page erases its history, tags and error reports. Its translations are separate pages and stay.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the GDPR in Europe, PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25 in Canada, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used. We can't access it, so send these requests to Root, which hosts it. Root's privacy policy explains how. You can also file a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

These apps aren't meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the European Union.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/brlehe98/newsroom-com/issues. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.
