Newsroom – Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Newsroom is an editorial app that runs inside Root communities. In short: the app keeps only what it needs to work, inside your community, and we, its developer, cannot see any of it. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

What the app keeps

  • What you create in the app: pitches, article texts, comments, suggestions, field values and the photos you add.
  • Your Root member ID, so the app can show who wrote what, credit authors and apply the access your community gives you.
  • Your notification preferences, if you change them.

Your name and profile picture come from Root and are shown as Root provides them; the app does not keep its own copy of your profile.

We have no access to this data. It stays in your community’s copy of the app, hosted by Root. We, the developer, cannot read, export, change or delete it, and nothing is sent to us.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no cookies.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No use of your content for any purpose other than running the app for your community.

Where the data lives

The app runs on Root’s infrastructure, and Root hosts its data and photos. Root’s own privacy policy covers your Root account. The data may be stored outside your country, including in the United States.

Why the data is used (GDPR)

The app uses this data only to provide the service your community chose (Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for things like keeping a version history, to offer a reliable tool (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

Content stays as long as your community keeps it. Deleted text tabs stay in a trash for a limited time (30 days by default) and are then erased. A deleted article is erased with its texts, comments, history and fields. If the app is removed from a community, its data is removed with it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, PIPEDA and Québec’s Law 25 in Canada, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it is used. Since we cannot access it, send these requests to Root, which hosts the data, as explained in Root’s privacy policy. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/brlehe98/newsroom-com/issues. Issues are public: do not include personal or private content.
