Newsroom – Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Newsroom is an editorial app that runs inside Root communities. In short: we do not collect anything about you beyond what the app needs to work. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, and we never sell or share your data.

What the app stores

  • What you create in the app: pitches, article texts, comments, suggestions, field values and the photos you add.
  • Your Root member ID, so the app can show who wrote what, credit authors and apply the access your community gives you.
  • Your notification preferences, if you change them.

Your name and profile picture come from Root and are shown as Root provides them; the app does not keep its own copy of your profile.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and no cookies.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No use of your content for any purpose other than running the app for your community.

Where the data lives

The app runs on Root’s infrastructure, and Root hosts its data and photos. The developer of Newsroom has no access to this data: we cannot see, export or delete your content. Root’s own privacy policy covers your Root account. The data may be stored outside your country, including in the United States.

Why we process it (GDPR)

We process this data only to provide the app your community chose to use: to perform that service (Art. 6(1)(b)) and, for things like keeping a version history, our legitimate interest in running a reliable tool (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep it

Content stays as long as your community keeps it. Deleted text tabs stay in a trash for a limited time (30 days by default) and are then erased. A deleted article is erased with its texts, comments, history and fields. If the app is removed from a community, its data is removed with it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, PIPEDA and Québec’s Law 25 in Canada, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to how it is used.

Because we have no access to the data, these requests are handled where the data is:

  • In the app: you can view, copy, edit and delete the content you have access to.
  • The administrators of your Root community can edit or delete any content, or remove the app and all its data.
  • For your Root account and anything Root stores, contact Root through its privacy policy.

If you are not sure where to turn, write to us and we will help you within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Ask the administrators of your Root community, or open an issue at github.com/brlehe98/newsroom-com/issues.
