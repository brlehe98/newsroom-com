Newsroom – Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Newsroom is an editorial app that runs inside Root communities. It keeps what it needs to work, inside your community, and nothing more. We built the app, but we can't see your data. There are no ads, no analytics and no cookies, and nothing is sold or shared.

What the app keeps

  • What you create: pitches, article texts, comments, suggestions, field values and photos.
  • Your Root member ID. The app uses it to show who wrote what, credit authors and give you the access your community set.
  • Your notification preferences, if you change them.

Your name and profile picture come from Root. The app shows them but doesn't store a copy.

What the app doesn't do

The app doesn't track you or show ads. Your content is used to run the app for your community and for nothing else. It isn't sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Where the data lives

Root hosts the app, its data and its photos. Your data stays in your community's copy of the app. Nothing is sent to us, and we can't read, export, change or delete it. Root's privacy policy covers your Root account. Your data may be stored outside your country, including in the United States.

Legal basis (GDPR)

The app uses this data to provide the service your community chose (Art. 6(1)(b)). Some features, like version history, rely on a legitimate interest in a tool that works reliably (Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long data is kept

Content stays until your community deletes it. Deleted text tabs go to a trash and are erased after 30 days by default. Deleting an article erases its texts, comments, history and fields. Removing the app from a community removes all of its data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the GDPR in Europe, PIPEDA and Québec's Law 25 in Canada, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used. We can't access it, so send these requests to Root, which hosts it. Root's privacy policy explains how. You can also file a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

Newsroom isn't meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the European Union.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/brlehe98/newsroom-com/issues. Issues are public, so leave out anything personal.
